Box Detective

What's in the box?

No phone can truly see through cardboard — so this works like a detective. You run a few quick tests, and it combines the clues into a ranked guess:

  1. Look — photos of the label & markings
  2. Measure — size + weight → density
  3. Shake — microphone listens for rattles
  4. Tilt — motion sensor feels things slide
  5. Knock — hollow or packed solid?
  6. Context — occasion & who it's for

Every step is optional; more clues = better guess. Takes about a minute.

3 · Shake test

  1. Put the phone down, mic end towards you.
  2. Tap start, stay quiet for a moment.
  3. Shake the box gently right next to the phone, 5–6 times.

What did it sound like?

4 · Tilt test

  1. Lay the phone flat on top of the box and hold it there.
  2. Tap start.
  3. Slowly tip the box left, then right, a few times (like pouring).

The motion sensor feels the tiny jolt when something slides and hits the wall.

Does anything move inside?

5 · Knock test

  1. Hold the phone's mic close to the box.
  2. Tap start, then knock on the biggest side with a knuckle 3–4 times, about a second apart.
